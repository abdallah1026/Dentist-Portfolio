01About

Careful hands. Clear explanations. A plan made for you.

Mohamed Ibrahim BDS, 2023

I am Mohamed Ibrahim Aziz El-Din, a dentist and graduate of Ahram Canadian University, Class of 2023, holding a Bachelor of Oral & Dental Medicine. My clinical approach is simple: understand first, explain honestly, then treat with precision.

Every smile is different. That is why I take the time to listen to what matters to you — comfort, function, or confidence — and build a treatment plan around your life, not the other way around.

I want you to understand what is happening in your mouth, and to know your options before you choose. Good care begins with clarity and continues with trust.

05Your visit

What to expect, step by step.

A dental visit should never feel uncertain. This is the journey I have designed — clear, calm, and free of surprises.

  1. 01

    Conversation

    We begin with an unhurried conversation: what bothers you, what you would like to change, and what matters to you about your smile.

  2. 02

    Diagnosis

    A careful examination of your teeth and gums, with X-rays when needed, so we understand the full picture before any decision.

  3. 03

    The plan

    I explain the available options, their time and cost, and together we agree on a plan that fits your priorities.

  4. 04

    Care & follow-up

    Treatment is carried out in comfortable stages, and we stay in touch after your visit to ensure the best result.
